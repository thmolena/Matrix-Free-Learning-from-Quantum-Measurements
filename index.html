Safety-Gated Spectral Reconstruction of Experimental Ramsey Fringes

A protected interpolation default and nested spectral gate are evaluated on 40,787 probabilities from 177 experimental fringes.

Mean held-out RMSE: 0.09146, versus 0.09434 for linear interpolation. All 22 accepted-model losses remain in the evidence record.

Paper · Reproduction · Trace-level results

The paper proves a conditional safety-gate bound and exact block-elimination equivalence, then reports state-stratified accuracy, every trace outcome, selection failures, solve time, and memory.
